Korean biotech company Alteogen disclosed on November 26 that it will receive a $40 million (about 53.8 billion won) sales milestone from MSD (known as Merck in the US and Canada) tied to sales of subcutaneous Keytruda. Here is what that means.

## The key facts

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Amount | $40 million (about KRW 53.8 billion) |
| Type | Sales milestone based on Keytruda SC sales |
| Payment | Within 30 days of the invoice, non-refundable |
| Previous milestone | $25 million in August |
| Cumulative | $65 million of up to $1 billion (6.5%) |

Pharm Edaily noted the amount equals about 24.9% of Alteogen's consolidated revenue last year (KRW 215.9 billion).

## What is a milestone payment?

In a technology licensing deal, a milestone is a lump sum paid when agreed targets, such as regulatory approval or sales levels, are met. Alteogen and MSD agreed on up to $1 billion in sales milestones. About $65 million has been secured so far, leaving up to $935 million, according to the reports.

## Keytruda SC and ALT-B4

Keytruda is MSD's immunotherapy drug, originally given by intravenous infusion. Keytruda SC (sold in the US as Keytruda Qlex) is an injection under the skin. The reports say the key to the switch is ALT-B4, a hyaluronidase enzyme Alteogen licensed to MSD.

## Sales trend

MSD's global Keytruda SC sales were $830 million in the third quarter, up about 79% from $463 million in the second, per the articles. MSD pointed to broader early-stage cancer use and more combination prescriptions with the bladder cancer drug Padcev. In July the US FDA approved that combination around surgery for muscle-invasive bladder cancer.

An Alteogen official said combination therapies should raise the value of subcutaneous formulations and ALT-B4. That is the company's own outlook.

## Keep in mind

Milestones are one-off payments, and future amounts and timing depend on sales. This is general information, not investment advice. For exact contract terms, see Alteogen's regulatory filings.
