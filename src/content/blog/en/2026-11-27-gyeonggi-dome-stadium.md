On November 26, Gyeonggi Province, which surrounds Seoul, picked Gwangmyeong and Suwon as its candidate sites for a multipurpose dome stadium that South Korea's Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism wants to build for both sports and concerts.

## What was decided

According to YTN, Gwangmyeong is the candidate for the large category (40,000 seats or more) and Suwon for the mid-size category (between 20,000 and 40,000 seats). Eight cities took part, including Hwaseong, Paju and Hanam, and were assessed through written review, presentations and site inspections. Six were eliminated.

## Why these two

- **Gwangmyeong:** praised for wide transport links, including KTX Gwangmyeong Station and the GTX rapid rail network.
- **Suwon:** praised for its closeness to Seoul and its role as home of a professional baseball club.

Paju's mayor said the city was disappointed after long preparation but would keep pursuing sports and cultural development, Newsis reported.

## This is not the final word

The choice only decides which sites Gyeonggi will submit. YTN says the province will send its results to the ministry by December 8, and the ministry plans to announce one large and one mid-size site nationwide at the end of December. Because candidates from other regions compete too, neither city is guaranteed.

## What is still unknown

The articles we reviewed did not give site areas, total cost, exact seat counts or a completion target. Funding shares between national and local governments and who will operate the venue would also need settling.

## What to watch

- Whether a Gyeonggi site appears in the ministry's end-of-year announcement
- Project cost and how it is split between governments
- How the unsuccessful cities respond

For official schedules, check announcements from the ministry and the province.
