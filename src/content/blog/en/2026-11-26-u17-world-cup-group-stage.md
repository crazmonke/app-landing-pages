South Korea's under-17 men's national team won all three of its group games at the U-17 World Cup in Doha, Qatar, finishing top of Group B and moving into the round of 32. Korean media report it is the first time a Korean team has won every group game at a FIFA-organized tournament.

## Group B results

| Match | Opponent | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Uganda | 3-0 win |
| 2 | New Caledonia | 6-0 win |
| 3 | Ecuador | 3-2 win |

Korea scored 12 goals and conceded two. After the first two wins it had already booked its place in the knockout stage, so the Ecuador game was about finishing the job.

## How the Ecuador game went

According to MBC, Korea beat Ecuador 3-2 thanks to a 22nd-minute goal from Nam I-an plus two own goals by the opposition. Winning even with qualification secured completed the perfect record.

## What the coach said

News1 reported that head coach Kim Hyun-jun credited a proactive, attacking style of play. He said the players should not be intimidated by anyone they face.

## Why it matters

- **A first for Korea.** Media outlets agree this is the first full-marks group stage at any FIFA-run tournament for Korean football.
- **Group winners.** Topping the group usually means a better position in the knockout draw.
- **What comes next.** From here every match is win-or-go-home. Check the tournament's official schedule for the round-of-32 opponent.

Youth results do not automatically predict senior success, but a run like this gives young players valuable experience on a world stage.
