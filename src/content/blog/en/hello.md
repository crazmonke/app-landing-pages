Welcome. This blog explains the stories people are searching for right now, quickly and accurately.

## What we cover

Anything that is trending: the economy, everyday life, society, entertainment, sports and tech. Each day we pick the keywords that are taking off and explain them.

## How we write

- We compare several reports and official sources.
- We separate confirmed facts from unverified claims.
- We add background and context that news coverage often skips.
- We list our sources at the end of every post.

If you find a mistake, please reach out through the [About](/en/about/) page and we will fix it.
